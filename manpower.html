Safety on site

Safety practices our teams follow

TBT (Toolbox Talk)

A brief safety meeting held with the technical team before starting any work shift to discuss hazards and safety rules.

LTT (Lockout, Tagout, Tryout)

Also known as LOTO. It is a life-saving safety practice where power sources are isolated, locked, and tagged before any electrical or mechanical maintenance begins to prevent accidental machine start-ups.
