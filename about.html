Meet our Founder & Director

Mr. Yogesh Vasantrao Nikam

Founder & Director, Yoganand Electricals & Automation

Mr. Yogesh Vasantrao Nikam is an experienced electrical engineering professional with 15+ years of industry experience across electrical maintenance, industrial automation, machine commissioning, and electrical contracting.

He holds a Diploma in Electrical Engineering and a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electrical Engineering. Over the course of his career, he has developed extensive expertise in industrial electrical systems, automation, maintenance, and testing.

Professional journey

From the shop floor to founding Yoganand

  1. Diploma Trainee Bosch, Nashik

    Mr. Nikam began his professional career as a Diploma Trainee at Bosch, Nashik, where he gained valuable exposure to industrial engineering and maintenance practices.

  2. Senior Maintenance Engineer Mahindra CIE, Nashik

    He subsequently worked as a Senior Maintenance Engineer at Mahindra CIE, Nashik, further strengthening his expertise in industrial maintenance and electrical systems.

  3. Utility Maintenance Head Blue Star

    He then served as Utility Maintenance Head at Blue Star, where he was responsible for utility systems and maintenance operations, gaining extensive experience in managing industrial electrical and mechanical infrastructure.

  4. Founder & Director Yoganand Electricals & Automation · 2019

    In 2019, Mr. Yogesh Vasantrao Nikam founded Yoganand Electricals & Automation with the vision of providing reliable, professional, and technically advanced electrical and automation solutions to industries and businesses.
