Check sheet

Machine preventive maintenance

Our standard 44-point check sheet, logged per machine and asset number with status and remarks for every point.

Electrical 22 points

  1. Incoming 3-phase supply main switch on/off operation
  2. 24 V DC supply voltage
  3. Earthing resistance & leakage voltage below 5 Ω / 2.5 V
  4. Panel cooler working condition: temp. monitor 28 °C
  5. Tighten all power circuit connections: MPCB, MCB, contactor
  6. PID controller on/off operation
  7. Temperature, flow & pressure switch working condition
  8. Hydraulic, servo & pneumatic valve on/off
  9. VFD cooling fan working condition
  10. Spindle motor vibration level check
  11. Induction motor noise level
  12. Hydraulic motor current
  13. Coolant flow motor working condition
  14. Turret motor brake operation
  15. Lubrication motor working condition
  16. All motor cooling fans working condition
  17. All sensors: analog & digital signal feedback
  18. Thermocouple operation
  19. Encoder / tacho feedback
  20. Fire protection circuit working condition
  21. Emergency circuit operating condition
  22. Terminal power & control connections: replace burnt TBs

Mechanical 22 points

  1. Incoming air pressure ≥ 6 bar
  2. Hydraulic power pack oil level
  3. Hydraulic pump output pressure
  4. Lubrication pump working condition
  5. Conveyor gearbox noise
  6. Conveyor belt condition
  7. Gearbox oiling / greasing
  8. Identify and close air leakage points
  9. All motors vibration check
  10. Hydraulic tank oil level: top up if low
  11. Oil line leakages
  12. Hydraulic valve operation
  13. Pneumatic valve operation
  14. FRL unit working condition
  15. Dial gauge pressure set as per standard
  16. Exhaust system working condition
  17. Air & oil filter cleaning
  18. Brake pad condition: replace if worn
  19. Door alignment & lock
  20. Fire protection air damper on/off operation
  21. Couplings, pulleys, belts & bearings condition
  22. Foundation nuts & bolts
